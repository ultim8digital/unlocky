App blocking demonstration

A continuous screen recording of Unlocky on Android. The balance remains at zero throughout.

Open the recording · Android privacy policy

  1. Choose Algebra as the app to pause.
  2. Read the separate Accessibility disclosure and choose Not now. Reopen it and choose I agree. Open Settings.
  3. Enable Unlocky in Android Accessibility settings and accept Android’s permission warning.
  4. Open Algebra with a zero time balance. The Unlocky pause screen appears.
  5. Choose Earn time to open the exercise choices, then return to the Android home screen.

App blocking is optional. Unlocky uses the foreground app identifier to enforce the selection on the device. You can disable it in Android Accessibility settings. Android Home, Back and Settings remain available.
