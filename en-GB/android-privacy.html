Privacy Policy

Unlocky for Android is published by ULTIMATE DIGITAL. There are no app accounts, advertisements, tracking or purchases. This build does not have Internet access and does not send app data to the developer or external servers.

Camera frames are processed on this device with the bundled Google ML Kit pose model to count repetitions. Frames are discarded after processing. Photos and video are not recorded, saved or uploaded.

App blocking is optional. With your permission, Android Accessibility reads the identifier of a foreground app only to compare it with your selected apps and show an Unlocky pause screen. You choose Earn time to open the exercises. It does not read screen text, messages, passwords or activity inside other apps. You can disable this service in Android Settings.

Your chosen apps, exercise settings, repetition history, earned minutes and PIN protection data are stored locally. Android app backup and device-to-device data extraction are disabled for this build.

Reset Unlocky in Settings removes your chosen apps, history, earned time and PIN. Android's Clear storage or uninstall also removes app data.

Unlocky does not transmit personal information from children or other users. Camera processing and app blocking stay on the device.
